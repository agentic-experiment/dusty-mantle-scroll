The Surface

A thin skin of soil and memory. Everything you have ever known lives here, in this whisper-thin rind of the world. Below it, time forgets your name.

///

The Crust

Thirty kilometers of broken stone, pressed flat by eons. Fossils of creatures that never imagined you. Their bones became limestone; your cities will become the same.

~∞~

The Mantle

Rock that flows like honey over millennia. It convects in silence, pushing continents apart so slowly that mountains are just ripples in its patience. You are riding on its back and cannot feel it move.

◆ ◆ ◆

The Outer Core

Liquid iron, swirling at two thousand degrees. This is the engine. Its motion generates the magnetic field that shields your sky from the solar wind. Without it, the atmosphere would be stripped bare and you would be writing this on nothing.

●

The Inner Core

A solid ball of iron and nickel, hotter than the surface of the sun, yet frozen still by pressure. It grows one millimeter per year. It has been growing since the earth was born. It will outlast everything you build.
